# X1 Christmas Campaign: "The Stress-Free Christmas Planner"

**Window:** 16 Nov 2026 to 10 Jan 2027
**Model (X1):** ad → lead → free content delivered daily → voluntary payment ("pay what it was worth") → paid extras
**Content rule:** everything is **printable or readable on a phone** (PDF pages people can print or fill in on screen).
**Budget approach:** test small, then scale only the ads that work.

---

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

Most adults find Christmas stressful: gifts, money, food, family, deadlines. We give away a **free 12-day Christmas Planner**. Starting the day after someone signs up, they get one printable/readable page a day, and each page solves one part of Christmas: a gift list with a budget, a spending tracker, card and postage deadlines, a Christmas dinner plan and timeline, a shopping list, a calm-week schedule and so on. By Day 12 their whole Christmas is planned. At three set points we ask them to **pay what it was worth to them**. For anyone who wants more, we sell a **Premium Planner bundle** and, after Christmas, a **2027 New Year Reset Planner**.

Why this one:
- **Huge audience.** Anyone organising Christmas, mostly women aged 25–60. That gives the ads plenty of room to scale.
- **The need is real and arrives every year at the same time.** People actively look for planners and lists in November and December.
- **The value is easy to see.** It saves time, money and stress, so people are more likely to pay voluntarily.
- **Every page is naturally printable or readable.** It needs no design skills beyond one good template.
- **It continues naturally into January.** The 2027 planner sells itself to people who just finished the Christmas one.

## 2. Calendar & phases

| Dates | Phase | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **16–22 Nov** | **Build** | Make all pages, the landing page, the email sequence and the ads. Test everything. |
| **23 Nov–13 Dec** | **Main lead push** | Ads: "Plan your whole Christmas in 12 days, free". Each person's 12 days start from when they sign up. Ads stop on **13 Dec** so everyone gets Day 12 before Christmas week. |
| **27 Nov–1 Dec** | Black Friday / Cyber Monday | Ad costs are high. Don't scale those days. The "budget tracker" angle works well here ("Don't blow your budget this Black Friday"). |
| **14–23 Dec** | **Last-minute push** | Switch to a shorter lead magnet: **"Christmas Day Survival Kit"** (dinner timeline + last-minute checklist + games list, sent all at once). |
| **24–26 Dec** | Pause | No ads. One warm "Merry Christmas" email. |
| **27 Dec–10 Jan** | **New Year** | Ads + emails for the **2027 Reset Planner** (budget, goals, habits). Final payment ask. |

## 3. The offer stack

| Tier | What they get | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 12 daily planner pages + Day 0 welcome pack (Christmas countdown calendar) | 0 | This is the lead magnet. Give them all of it. Don't hold anything back. |
| **Pay-what-it-was-worth** | Nothing extra. It's a thank-you payment. | Suggested 5 / 10 / 20, or any amount | This is the core of X1. The ask goes out on Days 4, 8 and 12. |
| **Premium Planner bundle** | 40+ extra pages: Christmas recipes, kids' activity sheets, gift tags & labels to print, a New Year's Eve party planner, a matching cover, an editable version | ~12–19 | Offer it from Day 6 onward. It's the main thing you sell during the campaign. |
| **Christmas Day Survival Kit** | Dinner timeline, roasting chart, last-minute checklist, family games list | Free lead magnet from 14 Dec, or a ~5 upsell for existing leads | Readable on a phone in the kitchen. |
| **2027 Reset Planner** | Budget for the year, goals, monthly pages, habit tracker, "pay off Christmas" debt plan | ~15–25, early-bird ~10–12 until 1 Jan | Main income after Christmas and the bridge into the next campaign. |

> Prices: use the currency of the country you advertise in. Test the suggested payment amounts. If most people pick the middle amount, raise all three.

## 4. Content: the 12 days

Each day is **1–3 A4/Letter pages**, with clean, printer-friendly design (works in black-and-white) that can also be filled in on a phone or tablet. Each page has a short **"do this today" (10 minutes)** box at the top.

| Day | Page | What it solves |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (on sign-up) | Welcome pack: Christmas countdown calendar + "how to use this planner" | Gets them started straight away |
| 1 | **Christmas budget** | Total budget split into gifts / food / travel / decor / going out |
| 2 | **Gift list by person** | Name, ideas, budget, bought ✓, wrapped ✓ |
| 3 | **Gift ideas cheat sheet** | 100 gift ideas by type (kids, teens, parents, colleagues, "has everything") and by price |
| 4 | **Spending tracker** | Track every purchase against the budget → **payment ask #1** |
| 5 | **Key dates & deadlines** | Last posting dates, delivery cut-offs, school events, work parties |
| 6 | **Cards & thank-you list** | Address list, sent ✓, received ✓ → **Premium bundle launch** |
| 7 | **Christmas dinner plan** | Menu, number of guests, dietary needs, who brings what |
| 8 | **Food shopping list** | Organised by store section, what to buy and when (frozen vs. fresh) → **payment ask #2** |
| 9 | **Christmas Day timeline** | Cooking schedule counting back from serving time |
| 10 | **Calm week planner** | 24–31 Dec schedule: visits, rest days, travel, who hosts where |
| 11 | **Family fun page** | Games, films, a cosy-night checklist, kids' boredom busters |
| 12 | **Christmas wrap-up** | Final checklist + "next year notes" page + preview of the 2027 planner → **payment ask #3** |

**Production tip:** build one master template in Canva (header, day number, "do this today" box, the page itself, footer with brand). Then each day is a fill-in, not a design job. Export each day as a PDF. Also make a mobile-friendly version (one column, large text) for people without printers.

## 5. Funnel & tech (keep it simple)

```
Ad (Meta: FB + IG; Pinterest optional)  →  Landing page  →  Opt-in (first name + email, optional WhatsApp)
     →  Thank-you page (Day 0 download + "watch your inbox tomorrow")
     →  Daily email Day 1–12 (starting the day after sign-up, 7:00 local time)
     →  Payment asks Day 4 / 8 / 12 → payment link (Stripe / PayPal / Ko-fi)
     →  Premium bundle (Day 6+) → Survival Kit (mid-Dec) → 2027 Reset Planner (from 27 Dec)
```

**Tools (use what you already have):**
- Landing page and email: Systeme.io, MailerLite, Kit (ConvertKit) or similar. It has to support a **sequence that starts from the sign-up date**.
- Files: put the PDFs on Google Drive or in the email tool's file storage. Put **one link per day** in the email. Don't attach files, because attachments make emails more likely to go to spam.
- Payments: Stripe Payment Link with "customer chooses price", Ko-fi, or PayPal.me. Use a normal checkout for the Premium bundle and the 2027 planner.
- Pinterest: planners and printables do very well there. Pinning the free pages costs nothing and brings in extra leads.

**Late sign-ups:** if someone joins after about 10 Dec, the 12 days won't finish before Christmas. Send them **days 1–12 together in one "catch-up" email**, or (from 14 Dec) the Survival Kit instead.

**Landing page sections:**
1. Headline: *"Plan your whole Christmas in 12 days. Free printable planner, one page a day."*
2. Image of the planner pages laid out (mock-up on a desk / on a phone)
3. "What you'll have by Day 12": budget, gift list, dinner plan, timeline, deadlines
4. "How it works": one page a day → 10 minutes → Christmas sorted
5. Short quote or result (after the first week, use real feedback)
6. Sign-up form + "100% free. No card needed. Print it or use it on your phone."
7. FAQ: Do I need a printer? (No.) What does it cost? (Nothing.) What happens after Day 12?

## 6. Ads

**Platform:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram) to start. Pinterest ads optional as a second test.
**Targeting:** start **broad** (adults 25–60, your country) with interests like Christmas, planners, budgeting, home organisation, family, cooking. Add a **lookalike from your previous campaign's leads/buyers** as a separate ad set. Once the pixel has 50–100 sign-ups, the broad ad set usually performs best.

**Creatives to make (minimum 8, so you have enough to test):**

| # | Format | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photo: printed planner on a table with coffee + wrapping paper | "I planned my whole Christmas in 12 days (free planner) 🎄" |
| 2 | Carousel: 5 best pages | "12 pages. 10 minutes a day. Christmas sorted." |
| 3 | Short video: flicking through printed pages | "Stop doing Christmas in your head" |
| 4 | Text-heavy static | "Free Christmas Planner: budget, gifts, dinner and dates, all on one page a day" |
| 5 | Budget angle (Black Friday week) | "Set your Christmas budget BEFORE Black Friday" |
| 6 | Dinner angle | "Hosting Christmas dinner? Get the timeline that works backwards from serving time" |
| 7 | Last-minute (from 14 Dec) | "Christmas Day Survival Kit: free, readable on your phone in the kitchen" |
| 8 | New Year (from 27 Dec) | "Christmas is paid for. Now plan a better 2027 (free budget reset page)" |

**Copy template:**
> 🎄 Free Christmas Planner: one page a day for 12 days
> Set your budget, sort your gift list, plan the dinner and never miss a posting deadline. Print it or fill it in on your phone.
> ✅ Free ✅ 10 minutes a day ✅ Your whole Christmas planned by Day 12
> 👉 Get your planner now

**Test small, scale the winners:**
1. **Test (first 3–4 days):** 2–3 ad sets (broad / interests / lookalike) × 3–4 creatives each, at a small daily budget per ad set. Change only one thing at a time.
2. **Read results after about 3 days** or about 2,000 impressions per ad: look at CPL (cost per lead) first, then click-through rate.
3. **Cut:** turn off any ad with a CPL more than 2× the best one.
4. **Scale:** raise the budget on the winners by about 20–30% every 2 days (bigger jumps reset Meta's learning). Or duplicate the winning ad into a new ad set with a higher budget.
5. **Refresh:** add 2 new creatives every week so the winners don't get tired (watch for frequency above about 3 and CPL creeping up).
6. **Judge on money, not just leads:** after the first payment ask (Day 4), compare **revenue per lead** with CPL. Scale hardest whatever brings the leads who **pay**, not just the cheapest leads.

## 7. Messages (email + optional WhatsApp)

Keep every email short and warm, with one clear link.

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Sign-up | Welcome + Day 0 countdown calendar + "reply 'YES' so we don't land in spam" |
| Days 1–12 at 7:00 | "Day X: [page name] 🎄" + link + one-line tip |
| **Day 4** | **Payment ask #1:** *"This planner is free and always will be. If it's already saving you time or money, you can pay what it's worth to you, even 5. It keeps it free for people who can't afford it."* + buttons (5 / 10 / 20) + "any amount" |
| Day 6 | **Premium bundle launch:** recipes, gift tags, kids' pages, NYE planner |
| **Day 8** | **Payment ask #2** (light, with a small result: "your shopping list is done") |
| Day 10 | Premium bundle reminder + "forward this to a friend who's stressed about Christmas" |
| **Day 12** | Wrap-up + **payment ask #3** (the strongest one: *"your whole Christmas is planned, look at everything you've done"*) + 2027 planner preview |
| ~18 Dec (everyone) | Christmas Day Survival Kit (free for all leads, as a thank-you) |
| 25 Dec | Short "Merry Christmas" with no ask |
| 27 Dec | "Christmas is done. Here's a free 2027 budget reset page" + early-bird 2027 planner |
| 31 Dec | "Early-bird ends tonight" |
| 3–10 Jan | 2027 planner launch + final reminder + final "pay what it was worth" for everyone who never paid |

**Rules:** thank every payer by name within 24 hours (reciprocity builds on itself), and never make the free pages depend on payment. The X1 model only works if the free part really is free.

## 9. Numbers to track (daily scoreboard)

| Metric | What it tells you | Rough target |
|---|---|---|
| Spend | | |
| Leads | | |
| **CPL** (cost per lead) | How efficient the ads are | Compare with your last campaign |
| Landing page sign-up rate | How good the page is | 30%+ (a free, timely offer should convert well) |
| Email open rate | Engagement | 40%+ |
| Day 1 → Day 12 clicks | Are they actually using it? | Keep above 50% of Day 1 |
| **Payment rate** | % of leads who pay anything | Track for each ask (Day 4 / 8 / 12) |
| Average payment | | |
| Premium bundle / 2027 planner sales | | |
| **Revenue per lead (RPL)** | Total revenue ÷ leads | **Must be greater than CPL** to scale |

**Break-even example:** if CPL = 1.00 and 5% of leads pay an average of 10, the payment asks alone bring 0.50 per lead. The Premium bundle and the 2027 planner must cover the rest and the profit. That's why they matter. Check RPL against CPL about a week after ads go live (once the first sign-ups have had their Day 4 and Day 6 emails), then decide where to scale.

## 10. Risks & how to handle them

| Risk | Fix |
|---|---|
| Daily emails go to spam | Authenticate the domain, send links not attachments, ask people to reply "YES" in the welcome email |
| People stop opening after a few days | Keep each page to 10 minutes, make subject lines specific ("Day 7: your dinner plan"), and show progress ("you're 2/3 done") |
| Content not ready in time | Days 1–6 must be finished before ads go live. Days 7–12 must be ready before the first sign-ups reach Day 7 (about 30 Nov). |
| Few people pay | Make the Day 12 ask emotional and specific, and push the Premium bundle (people get something concrete for their money) |
| Ads get expensive around Black Friday | Hold budgets steady 27 Nov–1 Dec and scale again afterwards |
| Privacy | Ask only for first name and email, add a privacy line to the form, and make unsubscribing easy |

---

## 11. After the campaign (10 Jan)

1. Export the numbers. Compare CPL, payment rate and RPL with your last campaign.
2. Tag everyone who paid. They're your warmest audience and the best source for the next lookalike.
3. 2027 planner buyers go into the next offer (e.g. a monthly budget/planning club, or a Valentine's / Easter planner).
4. Save the best quotes, page photos and winning ads for next Christmas, and launch about 2 weeks earlier next year (early November).
