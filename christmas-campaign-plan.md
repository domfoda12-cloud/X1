# X1 Christmas Campaign: "Phonics Advent Calendar"

**Window:** 16 Nov 2026 to 6 Jan 2027
**Model (X1):** ad → lead → free content delivered daily → voluntary payment ("pay what it was worth") → paid add-ons
**Audience:** parents of children aged 3–7 (the same audience as the last phonics ads, so we reuse what worked)

---

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

A **24-day printable Phonics Advent Calendar**. From 1 to 24 December the parent gets one printable activity a day: a 10-minute, Christmas-themed lesson on sounds, syllables, blending and enunciation. The child colours in a calendar door each day. It costs nothing to join. The calendar builds a daily habit, we're in the parent's inbox or WhatsApp every day, and each day the child makes visible progress. At three set points (Day 5, Day 12, Day 24) we ask parents to **pay what it was worth to them**. For anyone who wants more, we sell two optional extras: a **personalised "Letter from the North Pole Reading Elf"** with a reading certificate in the child's name, and a **January "New Year Reading Kickstart" pack**.

Why it fits Christmas:
- An advent calendar is a ritual parents already know. They don't need it explained.
- It has a hard deadline (1 December), so people sign up now instead of "later".
- It gives parents something calm for kids to do in a busy, screen-heavy month.
- Grandparents and aunts look for gifts then, which gives us a second audience: the gift bundle.

## 4. Content: the 24 days

Each day is **1–2 A4 pages** (black-and-white friendly), takes about **10 minutes**, and has a *"say it out loud"* step for enunciation.

| Day | Theme | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (on sign-up) | Calendar poster + parent guide | How to use it, 10-min routine, how to model sounds |
| 1 | **S**anta's **s**ack | /s/ sound, hear it at the start of words |
| 2 | **T**ree **t**ime | /t/ + /s/ review |
| 3 | **P**resents | /p/, sound hunt around the house |
| 4 | **N**ose of Rudolph | /n/, first CVC blending (s-a-t, p-a-n) |
| 5 | Snowman syllables | Clapping syllables (snow-man, rein-deer) → **payback ask #1** |
| 6 | **M**ince pies & **m**ittens | /m/, /d/ |
| 7 | **G**ingerbread | /g/, /o/ |
| 8 | **C**andy **c**anes & **k**ites | /c/ /k/ same sound |
| 9 | Elf's rhyme time | Rhyming pairs (hat/cat, bell/smell) |
| 10 | **B**ells | /b/, /e/, blending 3-letter words |
| 11 | **H**olly | /h/, /u/, read-and-colour |
| 12 | Halfway certificate | Review of all sounds so far → **payback ask #2** + Elf Letter launch |
| 13 | **Sh**ush, Santa's sleeping | Digraph *sh* |
| 14 | **Ch**imney | Digraph *ch* |
| 15 | **Th**ank-you cards | Digraph *th* (tongue placement for enunciation) |
| 16 | Sing-along | Tongue twister practice ("Six silly snowmen…") |
| 17 | **Ee** for tr**ee** | Long vowel *ee* |
| 18 | **Oa** for sn**ow**/b**oa**t | Long vowel *oa/ow* |
| 19 | Syllable sleigh | 2- and 3-syllable words, split and blend |
| 20 | Write to Santa | Write simple words using sounds learned (**Elf Letter order cut-off**) |
| 21 | Tricky words | 5 sight words: *the, to, I, go, no* |
| 22 | Reading a mini-story | 6-sentence decodable Christmas story |
| 23 | Show & read | Child reads the story to a relative (video moment, ask for UGC) |
| 24 | Graduation | Certificate + recap of every sound learned → **payback ask #3** + Kickstart preview |

**Production tip:** build one master Canva template (header, calendar number, activity box, "say it out loud" box, parent tip). Then each day is a fill-in, not a design job. The old phonics material can be reused for the base exercises.

## 5. Funnel & tech (keep it simple)

```
Ad (Meta: FB + IG)  →  Landing page  →  Opt-in (name, email, child's first name + age, optional WhatsApp)
     →  Thank-you page (Day 0 download + "join the WhatsApp group/channel")
     →  Daily email (+ WhatsApp broadcast) 1–24 Dec at 7:00 local time
     →  Payback asks (Day 5 / 12 / 24) → payment link (Stripe / PayPal / Ko-fi)
     →  Add-ons (Elf Letter, Gift Bundle, Kickstart)
```

**Tools (use what you already have):**
- Landing page plus email: Systeme.io, MailerLite, ConvertKit/Kit, or similar. Has to support **automation by sign-up date**.
- Files: host PDFs on Google Drive or the email tool's file storage. Put **one link per day** in the email. Don't attach files, because attachments hurt deliverability.
- Payment: Stripe Payment Link with "customer chooses price", Ko-fi, or PayPal.me.
- WhatsApp: a broadcast channel/community. Use it for reminders and photos, not as the only way content is delivered.

**Late joiners (important):** content runs off the calendar date (1 Dec = Day 1). Anyone who joins after 1 December gets a "catch-up pack" email with every day so far, then joins the daily schedule. The ads say "start any day, catch up in a weekend".

**Landing page sections:**
1. Headline: *"A free Christmas Phonics Advent Calendar: 10 minutes a day, 24 days, your child reading by Christmas Eve."*
2. A picture of the calendar poster and 3 sample pages
3. "What your child will learn" (sounds, syllables, blending, first story)
4. "How it works": print → 10 minutes together → colour the door
5. Short parent quote or result from the last campaign
6. Opt-in form plus "100% free. No card needed."
7. FAQ: age range, printer-free option (works on a tablet), what happens after the 24 days

> Keep the promise honest: say *"reading their first story"*, not "fluent reader".

## 6. Ads

**Platform:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram). TikTok is optional if one of you can film short videos.
**Targeting:** parents (25–45) in the target country; interests like early childhood education, phonics, homeschooling, preschool, parenting magazines; plus a **lookalike from the last campaign's leads/buyers** (this will probably be the best audience). Target parents only. Never children.

**Creatives to make (minimum 6):**

| # | Format | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photo: printed calendar on a table with crayons | "Swap the chocolate advent calendar for one that teaches reading 🎄" |
| 2 | Carousel: Day 1, 5, 12, 24 pages | "24 days. 10 minutes a day. Free." |
| 3 | 15-sec video: hands colouring a door, kid sounding out "s-a-t" | "My 4-year-old read 'sat' on Day 4" (only with a real quote) |
| 4 | Static, text-heavy | "Free Christmas phonics calendar. Starts 1 Dec. Get it before it's gone." |
| 5 | Gift angle (from 10 Dec) | "The Christmas gift that teaches them to read. Perfect for grandkids." |
| 6 | After Christmas (from 26 Dec) | "Kids bored already? 10-minute reading games for the holidays." |

**Copy template:**
> 🎄 Free Phonics Advent Calendar for ages 3–7
> Every day from 1–24 December you'll get one printable, Christmas-themed phonics activity that takes 10 minutes. Sounds, syllables, blending and a first little story by Christmas Eve.
> ✅ Free ✅ Printable or on a tablet ✅ Made for busy parents
> 👉 Get Day 1 sent to you now

**Budget and testing (adjust to your budget):**
- **23–25 Nov, test:** 3 ad sets (lookalike / interests / broad) × 3 creatives, about £/$10–20 per ad set per day.
- **26 Nov onward:** turn off anything with a CPL more than 2× the best ad set. Put money into the winners and raise spend about 20–30% every 2 days, not all at once.
- **Benchmark:** the last phonics campaign's CPL. A free advent calendar should get a cheaper CPL than evergreen content because of the deadline.
- **Lead-push end date:** pull spend back after about 18 Dec, except for the gift ad set. Restart 26 Dec with the after-Christmas angle.

| When | Message |
|---|---|

## 9. Numbers to track (daily scoreboard)

| Metric | What it tells you | Rough target (adjust to last campaign) |
|---|---|---|
| Spend | | |
| Leads | | |
| **CPL** (cost per lead) | Ad efficiency | Beat last campaign |
| Landing page opt-in rate | Page quality | 30%+ (free + deadline should convert well) |
| Email open rate (daily) | Engagement | 40%+ |
| Day 1 → Day 12 link clicks | Are they actually doing it? | Keep above 50% of Day 1 |
| **Payback rate** | % of leads who pay anything | Track per ask (Day 5/12/24) |
| Average payback amount | | |
| Add-on sales (Elf / Gift / Kickstart) | | |
| **Revenue per lead (RPL)** | Total revenue ÷ leads | **Must be greater than CPL** to scale |

**Break-even check:** if CPL = 1.50 and 5% of leads pay an average of 10, payback alone gives 0.50 per lead. Add-ons have to make up the other 1.00+. So the Elf Letter, Gift Bundle and Kickstart **matter**, and the Day 24 ask matters most. Check RPL against CPL on 5 Dec (after the first ask) and decide then whether to scale or cut ad spend.

## 10. Risks & how to handle them

| Risk | Fix |
|---|---|
| Emails go to spam with daily sends | Authenticate the domain (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), use links not attachments, ask people to reply "YES" in the welcome email |
| People drop off mid-month | Keep pages to 10 minutes, use WhatsApp nudges, share kids' progress photos, "catch up at the weekend" message |
| Meta ad rejection (children's content) | Speak to parents, don't make claims about outcomes, no before/after framing, no targeting of under-18s |
| Content not ready in time | Days 1–12 must be finished before ads go live. Days 13–24 can be finished during the first week of December. |
| Few people pay | Make the Day 24 ask emotional and specific, and push the add-ons (real items in exchange for money) |
| Data/privacy | Collect only the child's first name and age, add a privacy line on the form, and let people unsubscribe easily |

---

## 11. After the campaign (6 Jan)

1. Export the numbers. Compare CPL, payback rate and RPL with the last campaign.
2. Tag everyone who paid. They're the warmest audience and the base for the next lookalike.
3. Kickstart buyers go into the next program (e.g. "Spring Reading Club").
4. Collect the best parent quotes and photos for the next season's ads (Easter is the next natural "calendar" moment).
