Natal em Ordem: what lands in the inbox every morning, 1–25 December

All 25 daily pages, drawn the way the subscriber will see them. The blue handwriting shows a page being used, so you can judge whether each one is actually useful. Each page sits next to its delivery notes: the email subject, what the person does, why it works, and where the payment asks and paid offers go.

Every page has the same 5 parts

  1. Header: brand and "DIA 07 / 25" counter
  2. Title and one-line promise
  3. "Faça hoje · 10 min": the single action for today
  4. The tool: table, checklist, cut-outs or recipe
  5. Footer: one tip, plus "risque a casinha X" (cross off box X on the Day 1 poster), which keeps the daily habit going
